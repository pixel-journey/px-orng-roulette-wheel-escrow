# Education

`uint8 % 37` over the full 0..255 range is biased. Accepting only 0..221 (exactly `6 * 37`) keeps every pocket equally likely. Rejected draws hold escrow and reserve until a new random is delivered after `reopen`.

Straight payout uses 36× with a 2% house edge so the long-run expectation is negative for the player without needing American double-zero math.
